The InnovateHealth operating layer

Six layers. One clinic.

Not a feature list. A workflow. Each layer handles one job end-to-end, so nothing falls through the cracks between systems.

Layer 1

Voice

Answers inbound calls and classifies intent.

Powered by Breeze AI

Every call answered, 24/7. Breeze identifies why the patient is calling — booking, prescription, billing, or urgent — and routes it without putting anyone on hold.

0 missed calls

Schedule

Books, reschedules, cancels.

Follows your clinic's rules. Matches the right provider, fills open slots, and backfills cancellations automatically.

95% slot utilization target

Intake

Sends forms and tracks readiness.

Pre-visit forms go out automatically after booking. Patient data arrives before they do. No clipboards. No faxes. No chasing.

4x less manual data entry

Remind

Confirms appointments and reduces no-shows.

Automated reminders go out at the right time. Cancellations trigger instant backfill. Post-visit follow-up closes the loop.

↓40% no-show rate target

Pulse

Routes feedback and service recovery.

Captures patient sentiment after every visit. Flags dissatisfaction before it becomes a review. Closes the service loop quietly.

2s avg response time
