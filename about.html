Our Story

A healthcare operations company.
Built inside a real clinic.

We exist because independent clinics lose time, revenue, and care quality to fragmented workflows — not because they lack good doctors. AI is our tool, not our identity.

Founded by a practicing physician · Tested at Blessings Medical Clinic

The problem

The front desk was drowning. But the answer was never more staff — it was fewer loose ends.

At Blessings Medical Clinic, the pattern was the same every week: phones rang unanswered during peak hours. Patients filled out the same paper forms visit after visit. Reminders went unsent. Follow-ups fell through the cracks. The staff wasn't failing — the workflow was.

Dr. Nikhil Gohokar, the clinic's owner and a practicing physician, realized the problem wasn't a lack of effort or tools. It was that every tool lived in its own silo. The phone system didn't talk to the schedule. The intake forms didn't flow into the chart. Nothing was connected.

He didn't go looking for an AI vendor. He started building the connective tissue his clinic actually needed — a layer that handles calls, intake, scheduling, reminders, and follow-up as one continuous workflow. That became InnovateHealth.

Clinics don't need more software.
They need fewer loose ends.

Doctors should focus on medicine

The platform handles operational workflow so providers never need another dashboard to check.

The EMR stays the source of truth
